Professional Human Resources applications for **Odoo Community** and **Odoo Enterprise**.

## Available Modules

### 1. HR Attendance Control

**Technical name**\
`hr_attendance_ctrl`

Improve Odoo Attendance with office location validation and geolocation
control.

**Main Features**

-   Office location validation
-   Polygon (PostGIS) support
-   Geolocation detection
-   Attendance location history
-   Google / OpenStreetMap integration
-   Compatible with Odoo 18 Community & Enterprise

**Status:** ✅ Commercial Module

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### 2. HR Holidays Quarterly Accrual

**Technical name**\
`hr_holidays_quarterly_accrual`

Adds a new **Quarterly** accrual frequency to Odoo Time Off.

Employees receive **4 leave days every 3 months**, calculated from each
employee's **Allocation Start Date**.

  Allocation Start Date   First Accrual           Second Accrual
  ----------------------- ----------------------- ----------------------------
  01 Jan 2026             01 Apr 2026 → +4 Days   01 Jul 2026 → Total 8 Days
  15 Apr 2026             15 Jul 2026 → +4 Days   15 Oct 2026 → Total 8 Days
  01 Jun 2026             01 Sep 2026 → +4 Days   01 Dec 2026 → Total 8 Days

**Features**

-   Quarterly accrual frequency
-   Employee-based accrual cycle
-   Compatible with Odoo 18 Community
-   Compatible with Odoo 18 Enterprise

**Status:** ✅ Free Module
